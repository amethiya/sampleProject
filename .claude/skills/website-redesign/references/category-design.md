# Design notes by trade

What good real-world sites in each trade do. Use them with the assigned layout template.

| Trade | Visitors come to | Put up front | Photography | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant | See the menu, book, find the address | Menu link, booking/call, hours | Dishes and the room, warm light, close crops | Warm, confident; serif or strong sans |
| Café / bakery | Check hours, see what's on offer | Hours, address, menu | Daylight, counter, bakes, coffee | Friendly, light |
| Gym / studio | Timetable, prices, join | Timetable, join button, location | Real training, high contrast (gym) or calm and airy (yoga/pilates) | Gym: bold condensed type. Studio: light serif |
| Salon / spa / barber | Services and prices, book | Book button, price list | Portraits, interiors, results | Elegant (salon/spa), classic dark (barber) |
| Clothing / boutique | See the collection, visit the store | Large product/campaign photos | Editorial, consistent crops | Magazine-like |
| Retail shop | What they sell, hours, location | Categories, hours, address | Shopfront, products | Friendly, clear |
| Healthcare | Trust, services, contact/booking | Phone, booking, services, hours | Calm, clean, real practice photos | Reassuring, plain language, light palette |
| Accounting / finance | Services, credibility, contact | Services, phone/email | Office or team, city | Measured, corporate, lots of whitespace |
| Import / export | What they trade, where, how to enquire | Products/services, enquiry contact | Port, warehouse, products | Confident, clear |
| Local services (law, trades, studios) | What they do, area, contact | Phone, services, area served | Real work, office | Trustworthy, direct |

## What makes a redesign look professional

- The first screen answers "what is this, where is it, how do I act" in a few seconds.
- Real photos, large and consistent; no clip-art, emoji or icon soup.
- Clean lists for menus and prices: name, description, price aligned right with a dotted or thin rule.
- Consistent spacing (a 4 or 8 px rhythm), aligned left edges, few font sizes.
- One accent colour, used for actions only.
- Quiet: nothing moves unless the visitor acts, apart from one gentle fade-in.
